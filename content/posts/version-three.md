The new Radio4000 is now live on [radio4000.com](https://radio4000.com). 

There are many new features and improvements to check out, but amongst our favorites:

- Control multiple decks
- Broadcast what you're listening to live, and anyone can tune in and share your good times
- Listen to any radio in "auto radio" mode. The name is maybe a bit confusing, but really it's just a mechanism that plays all tracks in a channel on a fixed, shared schedule so everyone will listen to the same thing. Ala radio.
- Much easier search, filtering for tags, sorting of tracks
- Batch-editing of all your tracks
- Custom themes you can design yourself
